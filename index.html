About

Ranch Farm Simulator is a ready-to-use farming game for Unity. Open it, press Play, and you have a working farm: buy animals, feed and seeds from the store, carry deliveries to your pens and fields, collect eggs, milk and crops, sell them for cash, and upgrade your farm. Progress is saved automatically.

Features

Complete farming loop

Buy, deliver, carry, place, collect and sell, all wired together.

Store system

BUY, SELL and UPGRADES tabs with a cart, level locks and instant selling.

Data-driven items

Animals, feed, seeds and products are ScriptableObjects. Add content without touching code.

Pens and fields

Walk-in egg and milk pens, crop areas, and generic placement points.

Progression

XP, level-ups, unlocks and upgrade tiers for pens, fields and inventory.

Save and load

One JSON save file with atomic writes and optional AES-256 encryption.

Mobile and PC controls

Touch joystick and swipe-look on Android, WASD and mouse on PC.

Documentation

README and a full User Guide covering setup, architecture and customising.

Technical details

  • Unity 6000.3.13f1 or newer
  • Built-in Render Pipeline
  • Android (landscape) and Windows PC
  • Unity Input System + TouchControlsKit
  • Kinematic Character Controller player
  • Google Mobile Ads, IAP and Firebase integrated (bring your own IDs)
